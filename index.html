Research
Preprint · SSRN 2026
Empirical Construction and Cross-Market Applications of a Global Fear & Greed Index (GFGI) and Sentiment Divergence Index (SDI)

Constructs a volatility-weighted Global Fear & Greed Index fusing equity and crypto sentiment, and a Sentiment Divergence Index measuring cross-market behavioural dislocation. Replaces hand-drawn regime thresholds with a Gaussian Mixture Model on the joint (GFGI, SDI) space, followed by a softmax classifier for real-time probabilistic regime assignment (94.2% out-of-sample accuracy). The Crypto-Led Greed regime produces a statistically significant mean 10-day BTC return of +6.69%. A dynamic allocation strategy improves the Sortino ratio by 5.1% and Calmar ratio by 14.5% over a static 50/50 benchmark, while reducing maximum drawdown from −13.3% to −11.9%.

Epidemic Modelling · Finance

Optimized Multiplex SIR Contagion Model

Implemented a discrete-time SIR contagion model on a 4-layer multiplex financial network. Derived exact MLE estimators for layer-specific transmission rates, delivering 50–400× speedup via analytic shortcuts — reducing time complexity from O(n²) to O(n).

Gold FOMO Index

Developed a composite Search Volume Index of investor sentiment toward gold and other precious metals. Analyzed persistence and volatility linkages via AR(1), OLS, and quantile regressions, then benchmarked predictive power using machine learning models.
